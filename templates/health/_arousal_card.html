How far outside the training data today's readings sit

The model is an RBF-kernel SVM, which judges by distance in feature space. Readings outside the range it was fitted on do not degrade gracefully - they land wherever happens to be nearest. Each row is one feature: how many of today's windows fall outside the range seen in training, and how far today's middle sits from the training average, counted in training standard deviations.
