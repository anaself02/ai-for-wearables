Three different denominators, one row, so each dial says which it used. Steps and Active are your most recent day against your own median for the last {{ days }} days, not against a target: a full ring is a typical day for you, and the day on show is left out of its own median. A rollup arrives some hours after the day it describes, so a dial often lands on yesterday; each one names its day, and says when that day is still running. VO2 max is not a daily quantity and is not compared with you at all - the arc is where one imported estimate falls in the published norms for your age and sex, which is a decade-wide band, so the figure steps when you cross into the next one. Sleep score is Fitbit's, out of 100, imported once and never updated since.
