{% comment %} Fitness age, in the row, in a circle that is deliberately not a dial. The objection to a ring was that a ring is a fraction of something and a fitness age is not - drawing an arc would invent a denominator. That is an objection to the arc rather than to the shape, so there is no arc here: one circle, drawn at a hairline, with the number inside it. Standing in the row rather than below a rule, two things keep it from being read as a dial sitting at a hundred per cent. The stroke: everywhere else in this app 3px is data and 1px is structure, so a hairline circle is a frame rather than a track. And the rule down its left edge, which is the divider it used to sit under, turned ninety degrees - the same hairline the card grid is built from, saying the same thing in the same vocabulary. No caveat here, and no calendar age. The number carries a bound when the published table cannot place it, which is legible on its own; why it is bounded, and why it is partly circular, live on /fitness/, linked from the footnote under the row. The aria-label still says it, because a caveat that has been moved for visual quiet should not go missing for a reader who cannot see the quiet. {% endcomment %}
