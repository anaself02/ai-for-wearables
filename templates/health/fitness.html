What this is, and is not

Where the figure comes from

{{ assessment.vo2max }} ml/kg/min, recorded {{ reading.date }}, source {{ assessment.source }}. Google derives a demographic estimate from resting heart rate, age, sex and weight. No run, no treadmill and no gas analysis went into it.

What the percentile means

Of people of your age and sex in the reference population, about {% if assessment.position == "inside" %}{{ assessment.percentile|floatformat:0 }}%{% else %}this many{% endif %} have a lower VO2 max. The reference is the Cooper Institute Aerobics Center Longitudinal Study, as published in ACSM guidelines. Those are measured values; yours is modelled, so read the placement as approximate.

It is not a health check

Cardiorespiratory fitness is one measure among many, and a percentile against a reference cohort is not a diagnosis, a risk score or a medical opinion. Nothing here should be used to decide anything clinical.
