Your labels only grade the model. The model is trained on WESAD only; nothing is trained, tuned, calibrated or adjusted on your sessions.

How to run a session

  1. 10 minutes of seated rest (kind: Rest).
  2. 10 minutes of timed mental arithmetic, for example counting backwards aloud by 13, restarting after a mistake (kind: Stress task).
  3. About 20 minutes of seated recovery (kind: Recovery).

Only on yourself. Stop at any time if it becomes uncomfortable. Enter times in {{ person.timezone }} local time.

Limits of these labels: they are subjective (a task may not stress, rest may not rest); the body lags behind the label, which the {{ label_lag }} s buffer only reduces; and the same task stresses different people, and the same person on different days, unevenly.
