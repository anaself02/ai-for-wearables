How this is worked out, and what it cannot tell you

Where a cycle starts and ends

A cycle is measured from one REM onset to the next, which is the convention in sleep medicine. The stage timeline from the API carries real timestamps, so those intervals can be read off it directly.

The stages are themselves estimates

A wrist device agrees with clinical polysomnography on roughly 60 to 70% of epochs when sorting sleep into four stages. Every cycle length here inherits that error: this is an estimate derived from an estimate.

That proves REM is sometimes missed here. A miss that happens to land under 150 minutes cannot be told apart from one genuinely long cycle, so it is counted, and every one of those pushes the median up. The size of that bias is not knowable from this data, only its direction.
