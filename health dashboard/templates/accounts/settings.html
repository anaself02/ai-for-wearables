What happens to this

Stored encrypted

The date of birth is written to the database through the same Fernet-encrypted field as your Google tokens, so the column holds ciphertext rather than a readable date.

Used for one comparison

Nothing but the fitness percentile reads these. They are never sent anywhere: the norm tables are in the source of this project, so the comparison happens locally.

A date, not an age

Storing the date means the age used is correct the day after a birthday without anyone remembering to change it.
