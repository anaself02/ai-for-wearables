
## Amendment 5 - DRAFT, not applied (model x heart-rate resolution; to be frozen before any R2 result exists)

**Status: draft. Written 2026-11-01 before any R2 model result exists. Applies to the later stage named at the end of Amendment 4. Code: `wearable-ml/eval/` (`resolution.py`, `cross.py`, `interaction.py`). Amendment 4 is not changed: its grids, seeds, folds, metrics and bootstrap are used as they stand.**

- **Question.** Does the model ranking (SVM, RF, MLP) depend on the time resolution of the heart-rate input? Binary task (stress vs non-stress) only.
- **Factors.** Model {SVM, RF, MLP} x HR input resolution {R1s, R2}. R1s: native beat-level HR series (one value per beat). R2: the same beat series averaged into consecutive 3.0 s bins (bin mean, bins start at the window start, empty bins skipped, not interpolated). The two baselines (majority, HR_mean threshold, Amendment 4 rules) are run at both resolutions.
- **Features and windows.** The same five features (`HR_mean, HR_std, HR_min, HR_max, HR_slope`), 60 s windows with a 30 s step, windows 100% inside one condition (the original extraction rules, unchanged). **`HR_slope` is defined identically at both resolutions: bpm per second, the slope of a least-squares line fitted to the actual timestamps** (R1s: each beat's time, the midpoint of its inter-beat interval; R2: the bin-centre time of each non-empty bin). `HR_mean, HR_std, HR_min, HR_max` are computed on the beat values (R1s) or the bin means (R2) and, for R1s, are identical to the Amendment 4 R1 values. The per-beat slope of `features.csv` is kept only for the parity check and for the descriptive comparison below; no model sees it in this stage. A window is kept at R2 only if at least 10 bins hold a beat. **Both resolutions use the common window set** (windows valid at R2), so R1s and R2 cells are scored on identical windows. R1s is therefore re-run on that set.
- **R1 is rebuilt from raw WESAD** (`data/WESAD/S*/S*.pkl`, wrist BVP only; no personal data) with the project's own feature code and must match `results/features.csv` (same window set, same labels, every HR value including the per-beat slope within 1e-9). If it does not, the stage stops and nothing is run. Result of the parity check, written before this draft: 1015 of 1015 windows rebuilt, same set, labels equal, largest absolute difference 1.4e-14. At R2, 1012 of those 1015 windows have at least 10 bins (3 dropped: one each in S5, S16, S17).
- **R1s versus Amendment 4's R1, descriptive only.** R1s on the 1012-window set is compared with Amendment 4's R1 on 1015 windows **only descriptively** (no hypothesis test, no claim about a difference). The two differ in the slope feature and in three windows. Slope comparison on the 1012 shared windows (no model run): Pearson r 0.967, Spearman rho 0.993, SD per second / SD per beat 1.073, OLS coefficient of R1s slope on R1 slope 1.037, 7 windows of opposite sign; the other four features are identical.
- **Models, grids, folds, seeds, metrics, bootstrap.** Exactly Amendment 4 (grids, 7 inner subject-grouped folds, selection by mean inner macro-F1, ties to the first grid point, refit on 14 subjects, test subject predicted once; seeds 0-4; MLP max_iter=300, no early stopping; per-subject macro-F1 with seeds averaged first; 95% percentile bootstrap over subjects, 10,000 resamples, seed 6322). Grid search with 2 parallel fits; results do not depend on it.
- **Conditions.** `R1s`: train, tune and test on R1s. `R2`: train, tune and test on R2. `R1stoR2` (cross-resolution diagnostic): train and tune on R1s, test on R2. `R1s` and `R1stoR2` come from the same fitted models (one model, two test tables), so the transfer result adds no fits and tuning never sees R2. R2 never enters training or tuning of `R1stoR2`.
- **Pre-declared contrasts (per subject, seeds averaged first, paired bootstrap over subjects, macro-F1 is the headline, balanced accuracy and AUROC secondary):**
  1. Interaction, one per model pair: (MLP-SVM at R1s) - (MLP-SVM at R2); (MLP-RF at R1s) - (MLP-RF at R2); (RF-SVM at R1s) - (RF-SVM at R2). **These three are the interaction test.**
  2. Resolution effect: each model at R2 minus the same model at R1s (descriptive; 3 models, 2 baselines).
  3. Transfer diagnostic: each model at R1stoR2 minus R1s, and R1stoR2 minus R2 (reported separately from 1; the transfer-relevant case).
  4. Within each condition, the Amendment 4 pairs (MLP-SVM, MLP-RF, RF-SVM, each model minus each baseline).
  Nothing outside this list is claimed. CIs are not adjusted for multiplicity.
- **Ranking.** The order of the three models by mean macro-F1 is reported at R1s, R2 and R1stoR2. A change of order is descriptive only; the ranking is said to depend on resolution only if at least one interaction contrast (item 1) has a CI that excludes 0.
- **Claims.** A contrast is real only if its CI excludes 0. If all three interaction CIs include 0, the statement is "no interaction detected", not "no interaction". No winner is named when CIs overlap.
- **Outputs.** Per-window predictions of every run (`eval/out/resolution/predictions/`), `eval/results/resolution/{summary,differences,interaction,folds,failures,pooled_macro_f1}.csv`, one heatmap (3 models + 2 baselines x R1s, R2; mean macro-F1 with CI) as `heatmap_macro_f1.png`, a table, entries in `eval/EXPERIMENTS.md` (including MLP convergence per fold), and `eval/resolution_manifest.json`.
- **Limits, stated in advance.** (a) Averaging a beat series into 3.0 s bins does not reproduce Fitbit's smoothing, its motion handling or its beat detection; a device gap remains whatever the result at R2. R2 is a controlled change of time resolution only. (b) With 15 subjects the interaction test has low power: a difference of differences is estimated from 15 pairs, so the CIs will be wide and a null result is weak evidence. (c) The cross-resolution case also carries a shift in the feature distributions (at R2 the window mean is about 1.3 bpm lower and the SD smaller, because the bin means smooth the series); R1stoR2 measures resolution plus this shift. (d) The MLP reaches max_iter=300 in every fit (Amendment 4 run), so MLP results are for that fixed budget.
- **No retuning.** Bin width, minimum readings, grids, seeds, features and the contrast list are not changed after R2 results are seen.
- **Optional follow-up, not part of this amendment and not planned.** A check of the MLP with a larger `max_iter` could be proposed separately and labelled exploratory. It changes no result above.
